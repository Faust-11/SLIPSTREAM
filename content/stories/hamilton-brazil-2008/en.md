## The maths before the finale

On 2 November 2008 Interlagos hosted the 18th and final round of the season, the Brazilian Grand Prix.[^c1] Lewis Hamilton's McLaren arrived leading the championship on 94 points, with Felipe Massa's Ferrari on 87.[^c2] The equation was simple: even if Massa won, fifth place would be enough for Hamilton.[^c3]

For Massa it was a home race; for Hamilton it was a resit. A year earlier, in his debut season, he had lost the title to Kimi Räikkönen right here, at the final round.[^c32] Before the race local pranksters threw him a toy black cat, a symbol of bad luck. Hamilton was unfazed:

> "Where I come from, black cats are lucky."[^c5]

Massa took pole. Jarno Trulli's Toyota started alongside him, Räikkönen third and Hamilton only fourth.[^c4]

## Rain before the start

Rain arrived just before the start, and the race was put back by 10 minutes so the teams could change tyres.[^c6] On the first lap David Coulthard, in the last race of his career, and Nelson Piquet Jr were both out, and the safety car came out.[^c8] The track dried quickly and everyone gradually switched to slicks.[^c9] McLaren kept Hamilton out until lap 11, about two laps too long. He dropped to seventh but was soon back up to fifth.[^c42]

From there Massa led comfortably, while Hamilton held on in the points, repeatedly fending off Sebastian Vettel's Toro Rosso.[^c10] With 18 laps left Massa was about ten seconds clear of Fernando Alonso, and Vettel was 2.2 seconds behind Hamilton.[^c11]

## The last laps

A few laps from the end the rain returned, and almost everyone pitted for wet-weather tyres.[^c12] Toyota chose differently and left Timo Glock and Trulli out on dry tyres. Glock had been running seventh, so, as GrandPrix.com put it, he had nothing really to lose.[^c47] While the others were in the pits he climbed to fourth, and Hamilton found himself fifth.[^c13] Now he could not afford to let anyone past.

Robert Kubica seemed to have saved Hamilton: the Pole, a lap down, unlapped himself from Vettel and put some space between him and the McLaren. But a corner later he did the same to Hamilton, and Vettel swept through behind him.[^c43] So on lap 69 Hamilton dropped to sixth.[^c14] Had it finished that way, Massa would have been champion.[^c15] Hamilton said afterwards:

> "My tyres were shot. There was nothing I could do, I was trying to keep the car on the track."[^c16]

He started the last lap 0.754 seconds behind Vettel and 13.144 seconds behind Glock.[^c17] The rain grew heavier, and Glock, on dry tyres, could barely stay on the circuit:

> "It was almost impossible to stay on the track. I had no idea who was behind me and didn't see where Lewis overtook."[^c26]

## Thirty-eight seconds

Massa won the race. Alonso finished second, 13.298 seconds behind, with Räikkönen third.[^c18] Massa's family and team were already celebrating, but in Crash.net's words the elation lasted only a matter of seconds.[^c30] Hamilton crossed the line 38.907 seconds after Massa.[^c19]

In those seconds everything was decided. At Junção, the last corner before the uphill run to the line, Vettel and Hamilton caught Glock and both went past.[^c20] Glock's final lap took 1:44.731, Hamilton's 1:26.126: the Toyota lost more than 18 seconds in a single lap.[^c21] Hamilton finished fifth, Glock sixth.[^c22]

> "I couldn't believe it when I saw him at the exit of Turn 10. I knew I had to pass him in the final few corners."[^c44]

And so he did:

> "I managed to pass him in the last corner. I can only thank God. My heart was about to explode."[^c24]

After the flag Hamilton kept asking the team, "Do I have it? Do I have it?" He got the answer as he went into Turn 1.[^c25]

[[media:m3]]

Massa finished on 97 points, Hamilton on 98. The title came down to a single point.[^c23] Massa had in fact won more races that season: six to Hamilton's five.[^c37] But for that pass, Brazil would have had its first champion in 17 years.[^c36]

## "I know how to lose"

In parc fermé, still sitting in his car, Massa could not hold back his emotions.[^c45] On the podium he stood proud, just about holding back the tears.[^c46] He rated his own race without qualification:

[[media:m4]]

> "The race was just perfect. We did everything perfectly."[^c49]

And he described the seconds after the flag like this:

> "When I crossed the line the positions meant I was going to be champion, but then I was told Lewis had passed Glock."[^c34]

GrandPrix.com wrote that Massa took defeat with a grace and style rarely seen in modern sport.[^c41] He himself said:

> "I know how to win and I know how to lose."[^c29]

Glock had to answer questions about whether he had let Hamilton through on purpose. His reply was blunt:

> "People who think I would decide the championship or I would let Lewis pass, they do not have a real idea about racing."[^c27]

He explained that pitting for tyres would have made no sense: Toyota would have lost even more time.[^c38] Vettel, who had also passed Glock at the last corner, said he had no idea of his part in the title fight:

> "I was definitely not aware that I might be influencing the World Championship."[^c28]

Making sense of the chaos, he said, was hard even out on the track:

> "It was hard to know who was a backmarker or not and as it got darker and darker it was even difficult to know who the cars in front of you were."[^c48]

## The youngest, for a while

Hamilton became world champion aged 23 years and 300 days, the youngest in Formula 1 history at the time.[^c31] It was only his second season in Formula 1.[^c35]

The record lasted two years. In 2010, at the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, the title went to Sebastian Vettel, aged 23, and he became the youngest champion.[^c33] By Jolpica's dates he was 23 years and 134 days old.[^c51] Vettel's record in turn stood for 16 years: on 1 November 2026 Kimi Antonelli of Mercedes sealed the title with third place at the Mexico City Grand Prix, aged 20 years and 68 days.[^c39]

That same evening GrandPrix.com wrote that a finale this improbable would never have been made even in Hollywood: the scriptwriters would have been laughed out of the studios.[^c50] Later, Motor Sport magazine ranked the race seventh in its list of the 75 greatest grands prix.[^c40]
